Ludo - The Clean One Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Ludo - The Clean One ("the app", "we", "us") is a board game for mobile phones operated by Fishtail Apps. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

In short: your games, coins and settings are kept on your phone. The app has no accounts. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and we receive anonymous usage statistics and crash reports from Google Firebase to improve the game.

Information we collect

  • Game progress and settings. Your coins, level, achievements, daily missions, dice statistics, themes, house rules, settings and any game in progress are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They are not sent to us.
  • Player names. Names you type for Pass & Play are saved on your device only, to fill in the next game. They are never sent anywhere.
  • The dice. Each game's dice are decided by a secret key created on your device. The key and every roll stay on your device; the key is shown to you at the end of the game so you can check the rolls.
  • Usage statistics. The app sends anonymous game events to Google Analytics for Firebase, for example that a game started or finished, which rules and computer level were used, coins earned or spent, achievements and missions completed, and whether a rewarded video was watched. Firebase also records technical details such as an app-instance identifier, your advertising ID where allowed, device model, operating system, app version and approximate country (worked out from your IP address). We use this to understand which features people use and to fix problems. It does not include your name, typed text or contacts.
  • Diagnostics. If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, a crash report is sent via Google Firebase Crashlytics. It includes technical details such as device model, operating system version, app version and the error itself.
  • Advertising. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information and how you interact with ads. Where personalised ads are allowed and you have agreed to them, ads may be based on your interests.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and you do not need to create an account.

How we use information

  • To run the game and remember your progress and settings on your device.
  • To show ads, which keep the game free. Ads only appear on menu screens and between games, never during a game, and nothing you watch can change a dice roll.
  • To understand how the game is played and improve it (usage statistics).
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs (crash reports).

We do not sell your information.

Sharing

  • Service providers. Google provides AdMob (ads), Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics) and Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports). They handle information under Google's own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • App store. If you rate the game, that is handled by Google Play or the App Store.
  • Legal. We may disclose information if required by law.

Your choices about ads

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded videos are always optional: you only see one when you tap a "Watch ad" button.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to load ads and send usage statistics and crash reports. The game itself works offline.
  • Advertising ID — used by AdMob as described above.
  • Notifications (optional) — only if you turn on the daily reward reminder. The reminder is scheduled on your phone; no push service or server is involved. Turn it off in the app's Settings or your phone's settings.
  • Vibration — for short vibrations on captures and wins. Turn it off in the app's Settings.

Data retention and deletion

  • Your progress, settings and player names stay on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its storage, which deletes them.
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days and then deleted automatically.
  • Usage statistics are kept by Google Analytics for Firebase for no more than 14 months and then deleted automatically.
  • Information collected by AdMob is kept according to Google's policies.

Because the app has no accounts, we cannot link usage statistics or crash reports to you. If you would like us to delete any information connected to your device, email us and we will do our best to help.

Security

Usage statistics, crash reports and ad requests are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, please contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add new features. We will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
