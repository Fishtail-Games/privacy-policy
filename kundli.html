Saral Kundali Privacy Policy

Saral Kundali – Vedic Birth Chart (Kundli) · Effective date: 14 November 2026

Overview

Saral Kundali ("the app", Android package com.saralapps.kundli) makes Vedic birth charts, dashas, matching and panchang. Every calculation runs on your phone. This policy explains what information the app handles and what happens to it.

In short: the app does not collect, upload or share any personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not request internet access, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else.

Information you enter

To make a kundali, you enter details for each person you add:

  • Name and gender
  • Date and time of birth (or that the birth time is unknown)
  • Place of birth, chosen from a list built into the app, with its latitude, longitude and time zone

The app also keeps your settings, such as language, theme, chart style and calculation options.

This information is stored only on your device and is used only to calculate and show charts, readings and matching inside the app. We never receive it.

Location

The app does not access your device's location (GPS or network location). Birth places and the place used for today's panchang are picked by you from an offline list.

Sharing and export

Information leaves the app only when you choose to send it:

  • Sharing a PDF report or chart image opens Android's share sheet, and the file goes to the app you pick (for example a messaging or email app).
  • Exporting profiles saves a file to the location you pick. Importing reads only the file you pick.

Once shared, the file is handled under the privacy policy of the app or service you sent it to.

Device backup

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include your saved profiles and settings in your device backup to your Google account, so they can be restored on a new phone. This backup is run and secured by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your device's settings.

Third-party services

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking services, and no in-app purchases.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete a profile in the app to remove it from your device.
  • Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes all profiles and settings from your device.
  • Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox and your device's screen lock and encryption.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If our practices change, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app.
